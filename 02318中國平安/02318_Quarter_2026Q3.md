## **ANNOUNCEMENT OF UNAUDITED RESULTS FOR THE NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026** 

The board of directors (the “ **Board** ”) of Ping An Insurance (Group) Company of China, Ltd. (“ **Ping An** ” or the “ **Company** ”) announces the unaudited results (the “ **Third Quarterly Results** ”) of the Company and its subsidiaries (the “ **Group** ”) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 (the “ **Reporting Period** ”). The Board and its Audit and Risk Management Committee have reviewed the Third Quarterly Results. 

### **1. KEY BUSINESS PERFORMANCE** 

### **1.1 Business Highlights** 

China’s economy sustained steady development against pressure despite a complex, severe external environment in the first nine months of 2026. Key economic indicators manifested growing positive changes, showing development momentum with new growth drivers, improving structure, and better quality and efficiency. Ping An consistently enhances its integrated finance advantages and pursues high-quality development under a customer needs-oriented approach by advancing its tech-enabled “integrated finance + health and senior care” dual-pronged strategy. 

- The Group maintained robust overall business performance. The Group’s operating profit attributable to shareholders of the parent company grew 8.3% year on year to RMB125,905 million in the first nine months of 2026. The Group’s equity attributable to shareholders of the parent company was RMB1,044,366 million, up 4.4% year to date as of September 30, 2026. 

- Life and health insurance (“ **Life & Health** ”) business grew steadily by advancing its high-quality development strategy. Life & Health’s operating profit rose 5.7% year on year to RMB87,238 million in the first nine months of 2026. By consistently taking advantage of multiple channels, Ping An Life achieved synergistic multi-channel development, boosting non-agency channels’ share in new business value (“ **NBV** ”)<sup>(1)</sup> by 1.8 pps year on year to 36.9%. Regarding products, Ping An Life proactively optimized the mix of premium payment periods (“ **PPP** ”) by transforming long-PPP products. Long-PPP products’ share in the agency channel’s new business<sup>(2)</sup> increased by over 10 pps year on year, indicating steady growth in business value.

- Ping An Property & Casualty (“ **Ping An P&C** ”) sustained business growth with improving quality and efficiency. ### **Operating profit** 

Operating profit is a meaningful business performance evaluation and comparison metric given the long-term nature of the Company’s major Life & Health business. Ping An defines operating profit after tax as reported net profit excluding the following items which are of a short-term, volatile or one-off nature and others: 

- Short-term investment variance applies to Life & Health business excluding the part subject to the variable fee approach (“ **VFA** ”)<sup>(1)</sup> . This short-term investment variance is the variance between the actual investment return on the aforesaid business and the EV long-run investment return assumption. Net of the short-term investment variance, the investment return on the aforesaid Life & Health business is locked at 4.0%. Debt investments at fair value through other comprehensive income backing such business are measured at cost. 

- Impact of one-off material non-operating items and others, being material items that management considered to be non-operating incomes and expenses. Such impact in the first nine months of 2026 comprised revaluation gains or losses on the conversion values of USD and HKD convertible bonds issued by the Company and so on, which totaled RMB4,462 million. Such impact in the first nine months of 2025 comprised one-off gains or losses resulting from the consolidation of Ping An Health and Ping An HealthKonnect to the Group and the sale of Autohome, revaluation gains or losses on the conversion values of USD and HKD convertible bonds issued by the Company and so on, which totaled RMB-9,347 million.
