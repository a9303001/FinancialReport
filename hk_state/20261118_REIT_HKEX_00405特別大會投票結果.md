# **POLL RESULTS OF THE EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING HELD ON 18 NOVEMBER 2026**

At the EGM held on 18 November 2026, the EGM Resolutions in relation to (1) the CCPT Matters Requiring Approval; and (2) the proposed grant of the Buy-back Mandate, were duly passed by way of poll.

Reference is made to the circular of Yuexiu Real Estate Investment Trust (“ **Yuexiu REIT** ”) dated 30 October 2026 (the “ **Circular** ”) and the EGM Notice attached thereto. Unless the context requires otherwise, capitalised terms used herein shall have the same meanings as those defined in the Circular.

At the EGM, the Ordinary Resolutions set out in the EGM Notice to approve (1) the CCPT Matters Requiring Approval; and (2) the proposed grant of the Buy-back Mandate, were proposed and decided by way of poll.

As at the date of the EGM, there were a total of 5,447,906,681 Units in issue.

As disclosed in the Circular, members of the Yuexiu Connected Persons Group are or will be counterparties to the New Leasing Framework Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereunder. Pursuant to the REIT Code and the Trust Deed, each of Yuexiu Property and Yue Xiu agreed to abstain, and procured that other members of the Yuexiu Connected Persons Group abstained, from voting on the EGM Resolution relating to the CCPT Matters Requiring Approval. As such, the parties

−1 −

mentioned above which were interested or deemed to be interested in 2,876,916,803 Units representing approximately 52.81% of the Units in issue as at the date of the EGM, abstained from voting on such EGM Resolution. To the best of the Manager’s knowledge, information and belief, after having made reasonable enquiries, no other Unitholders were required to abstain from voting on the EGM Resolution relating to the CCPT Matters Requiring Approval at the EGM.

Accordingly, the total number of Units entitling the Independent Unitholders to attend and vote for or against the EGM Resolution relating to the CCPT Matters Requiring Approval was 2,570,989,878 Units, representing approximately 47.19% of the Units in issue as at the date of the EGM.

With respect to the EGM Resolution relating to the Buy-back Mandate, the Manager is not aware of any Unitholders that were required to abstain from voting on such resolution. Accordingly, the total number of Units entitling the Unitholders to attend and vote for or against the EGM Resolution relating to the Buy-back Mandate was 5,447,906,681 Units, representing all of the Units in issue as at the date of the EGM.

All Directors attended the EGM either in person or by electronic means.

The poll at the EGM was scrutinised by Tricor Investor Services Limited, the Unit Registrar of Yuexiu REIT. The Board is pleased to announce that the poll results of the EGM Resolutions are as follows:
