### **PROPOSED VERY SUBSTANTIAL DISPOSAL AND CONNECTED PARTY TRANSACTION**

The Board announces that on 29 October 2026 (after trading hours), the Trustee (as the seller), the Manager and the Purchaser entered into the Agreement for the disposal of the Sale Share (representing all issued share of the Target Company) and the Intercompany Balance by the Trustee to the Purchaser (which is a Substantial Holder of SF REIT) for a total consideration of HK$2,611,147,699. The Consideration represents a discount of approximately 13.7% to the Distributions Adjusted NAV of the Disposed Group of approximately HK$3,024,533,000 as at 30 June 2026, and approximately 18.8% to the Adjusted NAV of the Disposed Group of approximately HK$3,214,446,000 as at 30 June 2026, respectively. Following Completion, SF REIT will cease to hold any interest in the Disposed Group.

### **PROPOSED SPECIAL DISTRIBUTION AND PROPOSED 2026 SECOND INTERIM DISTRIBUTION**

The Board has resolved, subject to Completion, in accordance with the Trust Deed and the REIT Code for:

- (i) the Consideration, less the Related Costs, to be distributed in cash to the Unitholders by way of the Proposed Special Distribution of HK$3.17 per Unit on a pro rata basis to the Unitholders whose names appear on the register of Unitholders on the record date (except for the Purchaser which has agreed to offset its entitlement against the Consideration pursuant to the Agreement). Such Proposed Special Distribution is distributed purely as a result of the Proposed Disposal; and

- (ii) the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution of HK$0.11 per Unit as the second interim distribution proposed to be paid to the Unitholders (of which HK$0.06 per Unit will be an ordinary interim distribution determined by reference to the 9M2026 Unaudited Income and HK$0.05 per Unit will be capital in nature) to be distributed in cash on a pro rata basis to the Unitholders whose names appear on the register of Unitholders on the record date (except for the Purchaser which has agreed to waive its entitlement to the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution pursuant to the Agreement). The capital distribution of HK$0.05 per Unit is declared and made pursuant to the exercise of the Manager’s discretion to include capital in the distribution amount during an interim period in accordance with the terms of the Trust Deed, in order to accelerate the payment of the annual distribution to be paid to the Unitholders for the current financial year if Completion occurs.

Subject to Completion, the Unitholders (other than the Purchaser) will receive aggregate cash proceeds in the amount of HK$3.28 per Unit, comprising (i) the Proposed Special Distribution of HK$3.17 per Unit and (ii) the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution of HK$0.11 per Unit, which, in each case, will be paid within seven Business Days after Completion. ### **PROPOSED TERMINATION, PROPOSED LIQUIDATION, PROPOSED DELISTING AND PROPOSED DEAUTHORISATION**

Given the Disposed Group comprises all of the operating businesses and assets of the Group, the Board is of the view that, following Completion, the Proposed Special Distribution and the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution, it is not anticipated that any assets will remain in SF REIT, save for the remaining cash required for the purpose of payment of the Related Costs in an estimated amount not exceeding HK$10,620,000 and any previously declared but unclaimed distributions. Accordingly, SF REIT would have no remaining operating real estate assets to fulfil the criteria for operating as a REIT in accordance with the REIT Code, and therefore, SF REIT should be terminated and liquidated in accordance with the REIT Code, the Units should be delisted from trading on the Stock Exchange and SF REIT should be deauthorised in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements. As the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting are natural results of the Proposed Disposal, the Board understands that the Proposed Disposal should be treated as part of the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting. The Proposed Disposal, the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting are subject to the Independent Unitholders’ approval by the Approval Threshold (i.e. approval by the Independent Unitholders representing at least 75% of the votes attaching to the Units held by the Independent Unitholders voting either in person or by proxy at the EGM, with the number of votes cast against being not more than 10% of the votes attaching to the Units held by all Independent Unitholders in respect of the resolution relating to the Proposals) at the EGM.

The Board proposes, following the Proposed Termination becoming effective, to liquidate SF REIT in accordance with the Trust Deed and the REIT Code.

In compliance with 11.8 to 11.10 of the REIT Code and the Trust Deed, following termination of SF REIT, the Trustee shall, as part of the process of liquidation of SF REIT, oversee the realisation of any remaining assets of SF REIT by the Manager and the Trustee shall ensure that the Manager shall repay any outstanding borrowings effected by or for the account of SF REIT (together with any interest thereon but remaining unpaid) and shall ensure the proper discharge of all other obligations and liabilities of SF REIT.

All the Unitholders whose names appear on the register of Unitholders on the record date (in respect of the final payment of proceeds (if any) derived from the Proposed Liquidation) will be entitled to participate on a pro rata basis in any proceeds derived from the Proposed Liquidation available for distribution to the Unitholders after any creditors and the costs of the winding up (including those arising from the realisation of any remaining assets of SF REIT referred to in the preceding paragraph) have been paid. Save for the remaining cash required for the purpose of payment of the Related Costs in an estimated amount not exceeding HK$10,620,000 and any previously declared but unclaimed distributions, it is not anticipated that any assets will remain in SF REIT following Completion, the Proposed Special Distribution and the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution. If the Related Costs exceed the estimated Related Costs of HK$10,620,000, SF Fengtai has undertaken to SF REIT to bear the amount exceeding HK$10,620,000. If the Related Costs are less than the estimate of HK$10,620,000, the remaining amount of the Related Costs shall form part of the assets of SF REIT, and shall, in the course of the liquidation of SF REIT, be distributed to the Unitholders in the manner specified above.

### **IMPLICATIONS UNDER THE REIT CODE, THE TRUST DEED, THE LISTING RULES AND THE TAKEOVERS CODE**

### **Application of Note 7 to Rule 2 of the Takeovers Code and commencement of the Offer Period**

Pursuant to Note 7 to Rule 2 of the Takeovers Code, if a company proposes to dispose of its assets and there is a proposal to withdraw the company’s listing on the Stock Exchange, the Executive would normally apply Rule 2.10 and other requirements of the Takeovers Code. Therefore, the Proposed Disposal, the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting would need to be approved by the Independent Unitholders with the Approval Threshold at the EGM. Pursuant to the Takeovers Code, the Offer Period has commenced on the date of this joint announcement.

### **Application of the REIT Code, the Trust Deed and the Listing Rules**

Clause 26 of the Trust Deed requires any connected party transaction to be carried out in accordance with the provisions of the REIT Code, the Listing Rules (to the extent appropriate and practicable) and any conditions (including any conditions of waivers or exemptions from the operation of the REIT Code granted by the SFC from time to time) imposed by the SFC from time to time.

5

Under 8.1 of the REIT Code, connected persons of SF REIT include, among others, a Substantial Holder and its associates under the Listing Rules. SF Fengtai holds 300,355,741 Units (representing approximately 36.61% of the total issued Units as at the date of this joint announcement) and is therefore a Substantial Holder of SF REIT. Accordingly, the Proposed Disposal constitutes a connected party transaction of SF REIT under 8.5 of the REIT Code. As such, the Proposed Disposal is subject to the reporting, announcement and independent Unitholders’ approval requirements under 8.7A of the REIT Code. 8.7A of the REIT Code requires all connected party transactions to be regulated with reference to Chapter 14A of the Listing Rules.

As one or more of the applicable percentage ratios (as defined in the Listing Rules) in respect of the Proposed Disposal is more than 75%, the Proposed Disposal constitutes a very substantial disposal of SF REIT and accordingly, is subject to the reporting, announcement and Unitholders’ approval requirements under 10.10B of the REIT Code and Chapter 14 of the Listing Rules as applicable to REITs.

Further, pursuant to 11.1 of the REIT Code and clause 29.1(b) of the Trust Deed, termination of SF REIT shall be subject to Unitholders’ approval by special resolution at the EGM.

8.7F of the REIT Code requires that, where a Unitholder has a material interest in the transaction tabled for approval and that interest is different from that of all other Unitholders, such Unitholder shall abstain from voting at the general meeting. Under paragraph 3.2 of schedule 1 to the Trust Deed, where a Unitholder has a material interest in the transaction tabled for approval at a meeting of Unitholders and that interest is different from the interests of other Unitholders, such Unitholder shall be prohibited from voting his/her/its Units at, or being counted in the quorum for, such meeting.

### **3. PROPOSED TERMINATION, PROPOSED LIQUIDATION, PROPOSED DELISTING AND PROPOSED DEAUTHORISATION**

### **3.1 Rationale for the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting**

Given the Disposed Group comprises all of the operating businesses and assets of the Group, the Board is of the view that, following Completion, the Proposed Special Distribution and the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution, it is not anticipated that any assets will remain in SF REIT, save for the remaining cash required for the purpose of payment of the Related Costs in an estimated amount not exceeding HK$10,620,000 and any previously declared but unclaimed distributions. Accordingly, SF REIT would have no remaining operating real estate assets to fulfil the criteria for operating as a REIT in accordance with the REIT Code, and therefore, SF REIT should be terminated and liquidated in accordance with the REIT Code, the Units should be delisted from trading on the Stock Exchange and SF REIT should be deauthorised in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements. As the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting are natural results of the Proposed Disposal, the Board understands that the Proposed Disposal should be treated as part of the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting. The Proposed Disposal, the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting are subject to the Independent Unitholders’ approval by the Approval Threshold at the EGM.

In compliance with the REIT Code and the Trust Deed, upon Independent Unitholders’ approval of the Proposed Disposal, the Proposed Termination, the Proposed Liquidation and the Proposed Delisting, SF REIT shall cease to create, cancel or sell Units. Trading in the Units on the Stock Exchange will be permitted for a specified period following the EGM, following which the Board intends to apply for a suspension of trading of Units on the Stock Exchange until SF REIT is delisted. Details of the trading arrangement, including the last day of dealing in the Units and the latest time for lodging transfers of the Units to qualify for entitlement of the Proposed Special Distribution and the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution will be included in the Circular.

### **8. REASONS FOR AND BENEFITS OF THE PROPOSALS**

### **For SFH**

SF REIT was listed on the Main Board of the Stock Exchange in 2021, as the first REIT in Hong Kong holding a portfolio of logistics assets primarily in Hong Kong and the Chinese Mainland.

SFH, as the sponsor and a Substantial Holder of SF REIT, originally envisaged that SF REIT would serve as a long-term capital recycling platform, through which SFH could progressively unlock value from the relatively asset-heavy logistics property portfolio and effectively monetise existing assets.

Amid the current challenging macroeconomic landscape, SF REIT’s ability to raise capital for future investment and growth has been constrained, further compounded by the subdued trading liquidity of its Units.

From SFH’s perspective, the Proposals enables SFH to better coordinate its logistics assets, achieve greater flexibility in asset portfolio optimization, eliminate ongoing redundant administrative and management costs, while representing a compelling opportunity to optimize capital allocation and deliver immediate and certain value to the Unitholders.

### **For SF REIT**

Amid prevailing macroeconomic headwinds, the broader real estate sector has also undergone significant market adjustments in recent years, resulting in REITs listed on the Main Board of the Stock Exchange generally trading at substantial discounts to their respective net assets attributable to Unitholders. The Consideration should therefore be assessed not solely by reference to net asset value, but also in the context of prevailing market conditions and the performance and prospects of the Disposed Group. Having considered this and the other factors set out in this joint announcement, the Directors are of the view that the Consideration is fair and reasonable.

27

### **For the Unitholders**

### **Opportunity to realise investment in cash at a premium over the historical trading prices:**

The Proposals provide the Unitholders with an immediate opportunity to realize their investment in SF REIT at a reasonable premium over the historical closing prices of the Units. After Completion, the Unitholders (other than the Purchaser) will receive the Proposed Special Distribution of HK$3.17 for every Unit as a result of the Proposed Disposal.

While the Proposed Special Distribution represents a discount of 13.6% to the unaudited consolidated net assets attributable to the Unitholders per Unit of approximately HK$3.67 as at 30 June 2026 based on the total number of Units in issue as at the date of this joint announcement (after deducting the 2026 First Interim Distribution of HK$0.1215 per Unit and the Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distributions of HK$0.11 per Unit), the Proposed Special Distribution of HK$3.17 per Unit represents a premium of approximately 23.3%, 23.7% and 27.0% respectively over SF REIT’s average closing price (deducting Proposed 2026 Second Interim Distribution of HK$0.11 per Unit) for the Last Trading Date, 5 and 30 trading days up to and including the Last Trading Date.

### **Certainty of value and an immediate exit for the entire holding:**

The average daily trading volume of the Units for the 12 months up to and including the Last Trading Date was approximately 810,070 Units per day, representing only approximately 0.10% of the total issued Units as at the Last Trading Date. The low trading liquidity of the Units imposes difficulties for Unitholders to monetize their Units through executing on-market disposals (if at all possible) without affecting the prevailing Unit price.

The Proposals provide the Independent Unitholders with an opportunity to realise their entire holdings in one go, at a uniform price and in cash without incurring brokerage or other dealing costs and without the price slippage and execution risk associated with on-market disposals. This offers a level of certainty in value and timing that the Units have not been able to provide through the market.
