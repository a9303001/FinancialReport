### **RENTAL AND OCCUPANCY RATE**

During the third quarter, the office leasing market remained in an adjustment phase. Vacancy trends varied across regions, while downward pressure on passing rents persisted. During the reporting period, due to adjustments in leased area by certain tenants, the average occupancy rate of our office buildings decreased by 1.7 percentage points to 72.0%.

Our Grade-A office in Beijing demonstrated some resilience against the backdrop of weakening market occupancy. Onward Science & Trade Center maintained the positive leasing momentum from the previous quarter, maintaining a high occupancy rate of 92.0%, although its passing rent declined to RMB182.8/m². The occupancy rate of our Shenzhen Grade-A office New Times Plaza edged upwards to 65.5%, while its passing rent weakened only slightly to RMB140.5/m², indicating signs of stabilization.

The three Net Valley properties recorded lower occupancy amidst increased leasing competition. Technology Building’s occupancy fell from 50.7% to 44.2% after a major tenant reduced its space due to changes in its operations. Nevertheless, the property’s occupancy rate remained higher than in the first quarter, while its passing rent remained stable at RMB71.9/m². Technology Building 2’s occupancy edged down 1.6 percentage points to 83.3%, but also remained above its first-quarter occupancy level. Cyberport Building recorded declines in both occupancy rate and passing rent to 79.3% and RMB114.9/m² respectively.

Garden City Shopping Centre continued to deliver steady operating performance, with its occupancy rate rising by a further 1.1 percentage points to 99.1%. There are only two shop spaces left to lease out before we reach full occupancy.

CM+U 2 Austin Avenue officially commenced operations during the third quarter and achieved full occupancy upon its launch. This marked the achievement of its pre-leasing target and was a key highlight of the quarter.
