# 3Q26 Hong Kong Office Leasing Market Conditions & Trends | Newmark

Published: 2026-11-24

Demand from banking, finance and insurance firms supported rental growth for prime offices in Hong Kong’s core districts in 3Q26, while rents continued to fall in several decentralised locations.

Grade A1 and A2 net effective rents rose 2.9% quarter-on-quarter in Greater Central and 6.5% in Tsim Sha Tsui, while they fell 5.0% in Hong Kong East and 5.5% in Kowloon East. In Greater Central, rental growth for prime space contrasted with declines at some older properties. In Kowloon East, competitive rents helped attract occupiers, and vacancy fell to 17.1%.

Across Hong Kong’s Grade A1 and A2 office market, net absorption more than doubled from the previous quarter to 764,500 SF of net floor area, and vacancy fell by 1.6 percentage points to 12.4%. Newmark expects overall vacancy to decline further by year-end. Limited prime availability is expected to support further rental divergence, with room for prime rents to rise while less sought-after buildings may still struggle to attract tenants.

### Key Takeaways:
- Net effective rents moved in opposite directions: up 2.9% q-o-q in Greater Central and down 5.5% in Kowloon East.
- Banking, finance and insurance firms continued to compete for prime offices to expand or upgrade.
- Greater Central, West Kowloon and Kowloon East accounted for most of the quarter’s net absorption.
- Kowloon East vacancy fell by 1.7 percentage points q-o-q to 17.1% as occupiers took up space at competitive rents.
- All 312,000 SF of office space at Central Yards Phase 1 has been pre-leased ahead of expected completion in 2027.
- Newmark expects overall vacancy to fall further by year-end and rental performance to diverge further by location and building quality.
