## **DISPOSAL OF PROPERTY – 700 NATHAN ROAD, HONG KONG**

### **THE DISPOSAL**

On 30 October 2026 (after trading hours), the Vendor (being Link (HK) Holdings Limited, which is indirectly wholly-owned by Link REIT) entered into the Sale and Purchase Agreement with the Purchaser, pursuant to which the Vendor agreed to sell, and the Purchaser agreed to purchase, the Sale Share. Contemporaneously with Completion, the Assignor will assign, and the Assignee will take an assignment of, the benefit of the Sale Loan pursuant to the Deed of Assignment.

The Initial Consideration for the Sale Share and the assignment of the Sale Loan is HK$2,300,000,000 (which represents the agreed value of the Property), subject to adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement. The Appraised Value of the Property was HK$2,516,900,000 as at the Valuation Date (i.e. 30 September 2026).

Upon Completion, each of the Target Group Companies, and hence the Property, will be wholly owned by the Purchaser, and the Target Group Companies will cease to be subsidiaries of Link REIT.

**Completion is not conditional on the satisfaction or waiver of any condition precedent. However, the Completion of the Disposal is subject to the relevant parties ’ compliance with their respective obligations as provided under the terms of the Sale and Purchase Agreement and therefore may or may not take place. Unitholders and potential investors of Link REIT are advised to exercise caution when dealing in the Units, and are recommended to seek independent professional advice if they are in any doubt about their position(s) or as to the actions that they should take.**

### **SECTION I. OVERVIEW**

On 30 October 2026 (after trading hours), the Vendor (being Link (HK) Holdings Limited, which is indirectly wholly-owned by Link REIT) entered into the Sale and Purchase Agreement with the Purchaser, pursuant to which the Vendor agreed to sell, and the Purchaser agreed to purchase, the Sale Share. Contemporaneously with Completion, the Assignor will assign, and the Assignee will take an assignment of, the benefit of the Sale Loan pursuant to the Deed of Assignment.

The Sale Share represents the entire issued share capital of the Target Company and the Sale Loan represents the loan owed by the Target Company to the Assignor outstanding as at Completion. The Target Company indirectly owns the legal and beneficial interest in the Property.

The Property, known as “ 700 Nathan Road ” , Mong Kok, Kowloon, Hong Kong, is sold on an “ as-is, where-is ” basis as at Completion, subject to the Tenancies.

In arriving at its decision on the Disposal, the Board took into account Link ’ s portfolio optimisation strategy to prioritise owning and actively managing shopping malls and car parks in Asia Pacific and divesting non-core assets to create value for Unitholders. As the majority of the income is derived from the office segment of the Property, Link determined the asset to be non-core. The Board also took into account the Appraised Value of the Property as at the Valuation Date (i.e. 30 September 2026), and the prevailing market conditions. The Appraised Value was arrived at by the Principal Valuer having also had reference to the recent market transactions in Hong Kong. The Initial Consideration for the Sale Share and the assignment of the Sale Loan is HK$2,300,000,000, which is subject to adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement. The Appraised Value of the Property was HK$2,516,900,000 as at the Valuation Date.

At Completion, the relevant parties will enter into (i) the Purchase Price Adjustment Deed, the key terms of which are summarised below; and (ii) the Deed of Assignment between, among others, the Assignor and the Assignee, pursuant to which the benefit of the Sale Loan will be assigned to the Assignee with effect from Completion.

Upon Completion, each of the Target Group Companies, and hence the Property, will be wholly owned by the Purchaser, and the Target Group Companies will cease to be subsidiaries of Link REIT.

### **SECTION III. INFORMATION ON THE PROPERTY AND THE TARGET GROUP COMPANIES**

### **1. GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPERTY**

### **(A) Address and general description**

The Building (comprising the Property) is currently known as “ T.O.P This is Our Place ” or “ 700 Nathan Road ” (formerly known as “ Trade and Industry Department Tower ” ), No. 700 Nathan Road, Kowloon, Hong Kong. The Building consists of 2 basement floors (with connection to the Mong Kok MTR station), a retail podium from ground to the 5th floor (with connection to the adjoining Argyle Centre Phase I via the footbridge), and offices from the 6th to 20th floor. The Building is for use as shop spaces, restaurant, and office spaces and the Property has a gross floor area of approximately 284,767 square feet.

### **(B) Particulars of the Property**

The Property comprises 24,750 equal undivided 25,000th parts or shares of and in Kowloon Inland Lot No. 10470 and the Building erected thereon, and is held under the land grant for a term of 75 years commencing from 30 May 1980.

The Property was acquired by Link REIT (through Link Monte (HK) Limited) pursuant to a Government tender. Further details of the acquisition of the Property are set out in the announcement of Link REIT dated 19 February 2016.

### **SECTION V. REASONS FOR AND FINANCIAL IMPACT OF THE DISPOSAL**

The Disposal is in line with Link ’ s portfolio optimisation strategy which prioritises owning and actively managing shopping malls and car parks in Asia Pacific and divesting non-core assets to create value for Unitholders. As the majority of the income is derived from the office segment of the Property, Link determined the asset to be non-core. Link will continue to review the mix and diversity, and monitor the performance, of Link REIT ’ s portfolio.

The Initial Consideration for the Sale Share and the assignment of the Sale Loan is HK$2,300,000,000 (which represents the agreed value of the Property), which is subject to adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement. Subject to Completion and adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement, it is estimated that the net proceeds from the Disposal will be approximately HK$2,280,000,000 (after taking into account, among others, the Expenses).

It is estimated that the net loss from the Disposal is approximately HK$372,000,000. The estimated loss has been calculated on the basis of the Initial Consideration, as adjusted in accordance with the Sale and Purchase Agreement, less the book value of the Target Group Companies as at 31 March 2026 and the Expenses. The actual amount of the net loss from the Disposal will be subject to the completion adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement and audit.

Subject to Completion and adjustments under the Sale and Purchase Agreement, based on the consolidated financial position of Link REIT as at 31 March 2026 (as disclosed in Link REIT ’ s Annual Report 2025/2026) and after taking into account the impact of the final distribution for the year ended 31 March 2026 paid by Link REIT on 28 July 2026, the distribution reinvestment scheme in respect of the final distribution for the year ended 31 March 2026, the unit buybacks executed during the period from 1 April 2026 to the date of this announcement, the disposal of Swing By @ Thomson Plaza and the disposal of a 50% interest in 100 Market Street, and assuming Completion of the Disposal had taken place on 31 March 2026, the pro-forma adjusted ratio of debt to total assets of Link REIT is anticipated to change from approximately 25.6% to approximately 24.8%. The Disposal is not expected to have any material adverse impact on the financial position of Link REIT as compared with that as at 31 March 2026 (as disclosed in Link REIT ’ s Annual Report 2025/2026).

Neither Link nor the Trustee will charge any fee to Link REIT in connection with the Disposal.

Having considered the above, Link considers the Disposal to be in the interests of Link REIT and the Unitholders as a whole, and Link is satisfied that the terms of the Sale and Purchase Agreement, including the basis for determining the Consideration thereunder, have been arrived at after arm ’ s length negotiations and are fair and reasonable and in the interests of Link REIT and the Unitholders as a whole.
