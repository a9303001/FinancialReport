## **Provisional Visitor Arrivals for October Reach About 5.13 Million, up 12% Year-on-Year Arrivals for the First Ten Months Near 46 Million**

The **Hong Kong Tourism Board (HKTB)** announced today that provisional visitor arrivals in October reached approximately 5.13 million, representing a year-on-year increase of 12%. The National Day Golden Week, alongside mega events and festive celebrations such as the **International Lantern Spectacular** and the **Hong Kong Wine & Dine Festival,** as well as MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conventions and Exhibitions) events, sports events and concerts held across the city, attracted a large number of visitors and helped stimulate tourism-related sectors, such as dining, retail and hospitality. Meanwhile, major exhibitions and events held across other cities in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area (GBA) facilitated regional tourism and business exchanges, further supporting visitor growth.

By source market, the Chinese Mainland and long-haul markets continued to perform well in October. Among short-haul markets, arrivals from several Northeast Asian markets were higher in October last year as the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday fell in that month, affecting the year-on-year comparison. India, on the other hand, recorded significant growth, driven by its long weekends and MICE events held in Hong Kong and other parts of the GBA.

For the first ten months of the year, cumulative visitor arrivals reached nearly 46 million, up 12% yearon-year, reflecting the continued steady growth of Hong Kong’s tourism industry.
