### 🎯 Why Are Brazilian Equities Dividend Yields So High?（結構性解讀巴股高息，含 PBR）
- **來源連結**: https://www.investing.com/analysis/why-are-brazilian-equities-dividend-yields-so-high-200681654
- **發布時間**: 2026-06-07
- **作者**: Lucas Camargo
- **核心觀點與論述**:
  > "This share is heavily driven by Petrobras (NYSE: PBR / PBR.A | B3: PETR3 / PETR4), a state-owned giant that alone makes up 12% of the index. Petrobras is often used by government administrations to improve the federal budget through massive dividend distributions."
  > "Following the Ukraine-Russia war and the subsequent spike in oil prices, Petrobras became the second-largest dividend payer in the world… By distributing US$21.7 billion and reaching a 30% dividend yield, it anchored the high-yield profile of the entire Brazilian market."
  > "Over the last five years, Brazil's baseline interest rate (the SELIC) has consistently hovered well above 10% annually… it makes more financial sense for companies to distribute their excess profits directly to shareholders as dividends."
- **關鍵要點與分析**:
  - 結構性利多／同時是風險：PBR 佔 Ibovespa 約 12%，是巴股高息的核心；政府常透過巨額配息改善聯邦預算——高息的另一面正是「政府把 PBR 當提款機」的治理風險。
  - 歷史極端值提醒：俄烏戰後油價暴漲時 PBR 曾配 US$217 億、殖利率達 30%，成為全球第二大配息公司（超越 MSFT、AAPL、XOM）——屬一次性極端，不可外推。
    - **每股化參考**：US$217 億配息 ÷ 約 61 億 ADS ≈ 每 ADS 約 $3.6（該極端年度）。
  - 高息成因：巴西 SELIC 利率長年 >10%，成熟型公司難找到打敗無風險報酬的內部投資，傾向直接配現金；且回購在巴西較不流行（國企回購會提高政府持股比例、帶來治理風險）。
  - 多空判定：⚪ 中性（解釋高息的可持續性與其政治根源）。可信度：中高（結構性分析、數據可驗證）。
