## **Financial Results and Position** 

## 1. Consolidated Financial Results for FY2027 First Half 

## Financial Results 

Consolidated vehicle unit sales in Japan and overseas decreased by 25 thousand units, or 0.5%, to 4,758 thousand units in FY2027 first half (the first half ended September 30, 2026) compared with FY2026 first half (the first half ended September 30, 2025). Vehicle unit sales in Japan increased by 54 thousand units, or 5.6%, to 1,024 thousand units in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. Meanwhile, overseas vehicle unit sales decreased by 79 thousand units, or 2.1%, to 3,734 thousand units in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. 

The results of operations for FY2027 first half were as follows: 

Sales revenues 26,962.4 billion yen<sup>(an increase of 2,331.7 billion yen</sup> or 9.5% compared with FY2026 first half) Operating income 2,191.1 billion yen<sup>(an increase of 185.4 billion yen</sup> or 9.2% compared with FY2026 first half) Income before income taxes 2,954.4 billion yen<sup>(an increase of 476.2 billion yen</sup> or 19.2% compared with FY2026 first half) Net income attributable to 2,236.4 billion yen<sup>(an increase of 463.0 billion yen</sup> Toyota Motor Corporation or 26.1% compared with FY2026 first half) 

The changes in operating income were as follows: 

Marketing efforts an increase of 165.0 billion yen Effects of changes in exchange rates an increase of 685.0 billion yen Cost reduction efforts a decrease of 340.0 billion yen Increase or decrease in expenses a decrease of 20.0 billion yen and expense reduction efforts Other a decrease of 304.6 billion yen

## Segment Operating Results 

## (1) Automotive: 

Sales revenues for the automotive operations segment increased by 1,880.5 billion yen, or 8.5%, to 23,981.1 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 112.7 billion yen, or 7.6%, to 1,598.2 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The increase in operating income was mainly due to the effects of changes in exchange rates. 

## (2) Financial services: 

Sales revenues for the financial services operations segment increased by 431.7 billion yen, or 18.5%, to 2,762.0 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 21.9 billion yen, or 4.9%, to 472.3 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The increase in operating income was mainly due to increase in loan balance. 

## (3) All other: 

Sales revenues for all other businesses increased by 203.0 billion yen, or 27.6%, to 938.9 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 60.1 billion yen, or 87.3%, to 129.0 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. 

## Geographic Information 

## (1) Japan: 

Sales revenues in Japan increased by 1,239.2 billion yen, or 11.8%, to 11,752.5 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 136.2 billion yen, or 12.2%, to 1,253.3 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The increase in operating income was mainly due to the effects of changes in exchange rates. 

## (2) North America: 

Sales revenues in North America increased by 1,453.6 billion yen, or 13.9%, to 11,922.1 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 234.0 billion yen to 166.1 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The increase in operating income was mainly due to the decrease in expenses and expense reduction efforts. 

## (3) Europe: 

Sales revenues in Europe increased by 609.1 billion yen, or 19.4%, to 3,755.6 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half, and operating income increased by 18.4 billion yen, or 9.2%, to 219.2 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The increase in operating income was mainly due to marketing efforts. 

## (4) Asia: 

Sales revenues in Asia increased by 225.8 billion yen, or 5.1%, to 4,677.3 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. However, operating income decreased by 7.1 billion yen, or 1.6%, to 437.1 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The decrease in operating income was mainly due to the increase in expenses and others. 

## (5) Other (Central and South America, Oceania, Africa and the Middle East): 

Sales revenues in other regions increased by 331.0 billion yen, or 14.2%, to 2,658.2 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. However, operating income decreased by 0.9 billion yen, or 0.5%, to 200.9 billion yen in FY2027 first half compared with FY2026 first half. The decrease in operating income was mainly due to the effects of marketing activities.

## 2. Consolidated Financial Position for FY2027 First Half 

## Financial Position 

The financial position for FY2027 first half was as follows: 

Total assets decreased by 3,541.5 billion yen, or 3.4%, to 101,980.7 billion yen at the end of FY2027 first half compared with the end of FY2026. Liabilities decreased by 827.8 billion yen, or 1.3%, to 63,674.3 billion yen at the end of FY2027 first half compared with the end of FY2026. Shareholders' equity decreased by 2,713.6 billion yen, or 6.6%, to 38,306.3 billion yen at the end of FY2027 first half compared with the end of FY2026. 

## Overview of Cash Flow 

Cash and cash equivalents decreased by 3,175.1 billion yen, or 25.1%, to 9,484.4 billion yen at the end of FY2027 first half compared with the end of FY2026. 

The increases or decreases for each cash flow activity compared with FY2026 first half are as follows: 

Cash flows from operating activities 

Net cash flows from operating activities resulted in an increase in cash by 1,603.9 billion yen for FY2027 first half. Net cash provided by operating activities decreased by 1,340.6 billion yen from 2,944.6 billion yen for FY2026 first half. 

Cash flows from investing activities 

Net cash flows from investing activities resulted in a decrease in cash by 535.3 billion yen for FY2027 first half. Net cash used in investing activities decreased by 2,982.2 billion yen from 3,517.5 billion yen for FY2026 first half. 

Cash flows from financing activities 

Net cash flows from financing activities resulted in a decrease in cash by 4,207.8 billion yen for FY2027 first half. Net cash used in financing activities increased by 3,845.8 billion yen from 362.0 billion yen for FY2026 first half.
