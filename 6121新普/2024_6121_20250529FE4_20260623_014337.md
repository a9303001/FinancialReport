I. 2024 Business Report
(I) Results of Business Plan Implementation
1. Business Results:
The company’s consolidated revenue in 2024 was NT$80 billion, a decrease of 5.6%
compared to the same period last year. The main reason for this decline was the drop in IT
product prices. In 2024, the overall demand in the IT industry showed signs of recovery.
However, there was no strong demand in the end-user market, and the overall environment
remained one of oversupply, leading to a downward trend in product prices.
In 2024, the global notebook market showed signs of recovery. Despite ongoing and
intensifying geopolitical influences, the active promotional efforts by brands to stimulate
end-user demand, government subsidy policies, and the heated discussions surrounding AI
PC contributed to the recovery. As a result, global notebook shipments reached 172 million
units, a 4.2% increase from 165 million units in 2023. The shipment volume of notebooks
also met initial expectations, showing modest single-digit growth.
Global tablet shipments in 2024 reached 147 million units, growing approximately 11%
compared to 132 million units in 2023. The global smartphone market reversed the declining
trend seen last year, with shipments reaching around 1.22 billion units, a 9% increase from
1.12 billion units in 2023. The overall market prospects for non-IT application products light
electric vehicle (LEV) and the backup battery unit (BBU) are still promising. The
environmental factors impacting these markets are gradually dissipating, and LEV
shipments are recovering due to efforts to manage inventory, while BBU shipments have
increased due to rapid demand growth in servers.

II. Summary of Annual Business Plan for 2024
(I) Operating Objectives
1. Continue to strive for technical and quality advancement on the basis of satisfying client
needs.
2. The objective of product design will be focused on eco-friendliness as we continue to
develop new products, expand innovative technology, and serve as a pioneer for the
industry so we can give back to society.
3. To increase our global market share, we will continue to cultivate our existing customer
base and proactively expand our international clientele for electronic devices that use
battery products other than laptop.
4. Continuously strengthen the partnership with key component suppliers, develop new
strategic collaboration plans, and, with the support of alliance partners, ensure a stable
supply of high-quality materials and competitive costs.
5. Continue to form strategic alliances and partnership with professional teams to expand
and solidify our existing market share.
6. Through strengthening our organizational functionality and utilization of human
resources, we can continuously enhance our overall business performance, productivity,
and profitability.
7. Faced with challenges such as climate change and accelerated global warming, Simplo,
together with its external upstream and downstream partners, is committed to energy
conservation and carbon reduction, and actively promotes carbon reduction initiatives as
well as fulfills its corporate social responsibility.
8. By keeping abreast of market trends, close internal and external communications,
production and sales management, optimizing production flexibility, and planning
overseas deployment, we can spread risks and reduce the impact of global economic
fluctuations and geopolitical tensions.
(II) Estimated Sales Quantity and Related Information
1. Estimated sales quantity:
In 2024, the global notebook market showed signs of recovery, growing
approximately 4.2% compared to 165 million units in 2023. Looking ahead to 2025,
various notebook brands are launching new AI PC products to stimulate upgrade
demand. Additionally, the termination of support for Windows 10, as well as demand
from educational tenders, are expected to continue driving growth momentum in the
notebook marketThe global laptop growth rate is estimated to be 5% in 2025.
In the global tablet PCs market, driven by the upgrade trend in 2025, various
brands are continuously optimizing their products and enhancing specifications to
meet the diverse market demands. This is expected to propel the tablet market into a
new development phase, with an estimated growth rate of about 3%. After global
smartphone sales returned to a growth trajectory, it is estimated that sales will
continue to grow in 2025, though the growth rate will likely slow down. Overall, the
IT market is gradually moving toward a steady recovery and growth. However, the
impact of policies such as the "America First" initiative, implemented during Trump’s
presidency, including the potential effect of increased import tariffs on domestic
demand in the U.S., still requires close observation.

In the development of new businesses, energy conservation and carbon
reduction, energy transition and green energy are still the future trends. The markets
of light electric vehicles, large-scale data centers and energy storage systems are all
showing steady growth. It is expected that the overall development of Simplo Group
in the new business in 2025 is still optimistic.
2. Basis of sales:
According to a survey by a market research agency, the laptop, tablet PCs and
smartphone shipment will show slightly growth in 2025. Among them, under the
application of AI in laptop, AI PC will drive the improvement of specification
requirements, which will be the main key of growth momentum. The tablet PCs and
smartphone are expected to continue their growth trend as brands continue to
optimize their products and improve specifications. Non-IT application products LEV
and BBU are still growing markets. After inventory adjustments, the LEV market
demand is expected to show healthy growth in 2025. The application of BBU
products will benefit from the booming development of AI applications, as cloud
service providers increase capital expenditures to build AI computing power. This
will drive significant growth in the AI server industry, which is also anticipated to
lead to a noticeable increase in the shipment of lithium batteries for large-scale data
centers.Therefore, it is anticipated that both IT and non-IT product sales for Simplo in
2025 will have opportunities for growth compared to 2024.
The Simplo Group has been deeply involved in lithium battery modules for
many years, becoming a leader in lithium battery modules for information products
such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones, continuously achieving stable profits and
accumulating cash. In addition to paying dividends to shareholders every six months,
the company also invests in new businesses and actively transforms. In 2025, with
end-user demand showing signs of recovery, the Simplo Group will continue to
accelerate new business transformation and overseas layouts to maintain stable profits
and growth through diversified product development and operations.
(III) Important Sales and Production Policies
In response to the effects of external competition, legal regulations and overall business
environment, the Company has proposed the following strategies:
1. Based on long-term risk diversification considerations, plans and layouts for
manufacturing outside of mainland China have been implemented.
2. Strengthen the R&D process and enhance technical competencies in order to boost
productivity and production quality.
3. Strengthen the utilization of the KPI Indicator Performance Management System to
facilitate locating, fostering, and improving the Company's core competitive strengths.
This will help us create more wealth and implement preventative procedures to
correct/prevent errors. In this way, we will continuously grow so that our Company may
operate in a stable, sustainable manner and satisfy clients and shareholders.
4. We will integrate supply conditions and production needs between the Company and
factories through globalized business operations strategies. This will provide customer-

oriented services.
5. Continue to commit to the R&D of new technologies and new products to maintain our
competitive edge.
6. Develop and innovate value-added products and implement the results of our R&D in
practice.
7. Include sustainable development as one of the Company’s core competitive strengths and
formulate development strategies in line with the government policies, regulations, and
customer needs to fulfill corporate social responsibility.
8. Implement environmental protection and carbon reduction practices, such as green
design, green production, and green recycling, into product research, development, and
manufacturing.
9. Enhance the ratio of automated production to create greater quality, competitiveness, and
productivity.
10. Foster international marketing talent, develop new international clients and increase
market share.
11. Cultivate more diverse battery supplies to support product requirements from customized
production lines.
12. Continue to develop diverse products apart from laptop, tablet PCs, and smartphones.
13. Implementing Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools and applications to assist with data
analysis and forecasting, improving efficiency and problem-solving capabilities, thereby
enhancing the company's competitiveness.
III. Future Development Strategies
In addition to the existing battery modules for information and communication
technology products, Simplo Group has also been actively developing new applications
with growth potential in recent years. In terms of power battery products, the company is
currently focused on the design, production, and sales of high-power lithium battery
modules for electric bicycles and electric motorcycles. The market share of two-wheeled
electric vehicles is steadily growing, and with the increasing environmental awareness, the
two-wheeled electric vehicle market is expected to maintain its growth.
Furthermore, with the rapid rise in demand for 5G, smart cloud, and public cloud
services, there has been an increasing need for the replacement of lead-acid batteries with
lithium batteries in large data centers. Simplo Group has been actively involved in this field
for many years and has established close communication and collaboration with leading
international brand customers. This will be an important growth engine for Simplo Group
in the coming years.
In response to the volatility of international affairs and the high uncertainty in industry
demand, the focus of future work will be to closely cooperate with customers, strictly
control inventory levels, and maintain our position in the IT industry. Additionally, in line
with the international trend of carbon reduction and government policies promoting
renewable energy, we will continue to invest in and deploy energy storage products. This
will not only be an important step for the group's diversification strategy but also contribute
to the stability of Taiwan's electricity supply.

IV. Effect of External Competition, Legal Regulations, and Overall Business Environment
The high mobility and productivity of laptops have become one of the essential items in
daily life. With the demand driven by AI, the demand for laptops is expected to increase,
which has also attracted many battery cell manufacturers to enter the competition for laptop
battery modules. In this regard, Simplo believes that regardless of how the external
competitive environment changes, the key to success is to continually strengthen its own
competitiveness and provide customers with meaningful value-added services.
Corporate sustainability is a crucial issue that enterprises must actively address. Simplo
Group aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and
implements ESG practices in its core business. While pursuing operational growth, the
company also considers the three dimensions of Environmental, Social, and Governance
(ESG) to strengthen corporate competitiveness.
Sustainable development has become an important topic for companies in recent years,
including "Corporate Social Responsibility" (CSR) and "Environmental, Social, and
Governance" (ESG). The "Paris Climate Agreement," adopted by 195 member countries of
the United Nations in 2015, sets clear goals to limit the global average temperature increase to
below 2°C above pre-industrial levels, with an effort to restrict the temperature increase to
within 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels. Therefore, setting scientifically-based carbon
reduction targets (SBTi) is seen as the first step for companies toward sustainable
development. Simplo Group takes this trend very seriously and has already actively
participated in it.
The Science Based Targets Initiative (SBTi), proposed by the Carbon Disclosure Project
(CDP), the United Nations Global Compact (UN Global Compact), the World Resources
Institute, and the World Wildlife Fund, aims to provide tools to help companies align their
carbon reduction targets with climate science. Simplo Technology officially submitted its 10-
year carbon reduction target to SBTi in September 2022, using 2020 as the baseline year and
setting a reduction target under the scenario of controlling global temperature increase to
1.5°C. The company will also include key suppliers in the scope of assessment and reduction,
working together with the value chain to take carbon reduction actions and jointly create a
sustainable and mutually beneficial ecosystem.
Simplo Technology has published its Sustainability Report, following the GRI Standards
and meeting the AA1000 ASv3 (AccountAbility) moderate assurance level. In response to the
low-carbon product trend, the company has completed ISO 14067 product carbon footprint
certification for IT battery modules, becoming the world’s first battery module manufacturer
to receive dual international sustainability certifications. In addition, at the beginning of 2024,
Simplo Group officially joined the global renewable energy initiative "RE100," led by The
Climate Group and the Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP), committing to achieving 100%
renewable energy use by 2040. Simplo became the first battery module manufacturer to join
RE100, showcasing the company’s sustainable competitiveness, enhancing ESG information
disclosure, and setting a new standard for sustainability.

selected on a meritocracy basis and have diverse and complementary capabilities across industries, including basic
components (e.g. Age, sex, nationality, etc.), and have industry experience and relevant skills respectively, and
business judgment, operation management, leadership decision-making and crisis management. In order to

processing methods.
