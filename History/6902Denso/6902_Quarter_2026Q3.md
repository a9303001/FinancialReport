## Summary of Management Results (Nine Months Ended December 31, 2025)

For the nine months ended December 31, 2025, revenue increased by ¥207.1 billion or 3.9% YoY to ¥5,495.5 billion, mainly due to the increase in vehicle sales of major customers and price adjustments, despite the impact of the strong yen. Operating profit decreased by ¥25.7 billion or 6.4% YoY to ¥375.9 billion due to U.S. tariffs, rising cost of materials and future investments, despite cost-reduction efforts and production volume increase. Profit before income taxes decreased by ¥10.1 billion or 2.3% YoY to ¥437.8 billion. Profit for the period decreased by ¥29.0 billion or 8.6% YoY to ¥308.8 billion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent company decreased by ¥39.1 billion or 12.5% to ¥273.7 billion.

By geographical segment:
- Japan: revenue +¥103.3bn (+3.3%) YoY to ¥3,225.1bn; operating profit -¥60.7bn (-34.2%) to ¥117.0bn due to rising cost of materials and future investments.
- North America: revenue +¥109.1bn (+8.0%) to ¥1,475.5bn; operating profit +¥2.4bn (+3.3%) to ¥74.9bn despite tariff impact.
- Europe: revenue +¥21.0bn (+3.9%) to ¥557.7bn; operating profit +¥12.1bn (+246.8%) to ¥17.0bn.
- Asia: revenue +¥4.0bn (+0.3%) to ¥1,466.9bn; operating profit +¥19.6bn (+15.6%) to ¥145.4bn.
- Other regions: revenue +¥1.9bn (+2.1%) to ¥91.8bn; operating profit -¥0.5bn (-2.8%) to ¥18.1bn.

## Summary of Financial Position

Total assets as of December 31, 2025 increased by ¥524.1 billion to ¥8,649.1 billion vs prior year-end, mainly due to an increase in other financial assets. Total liabilities increased by ¥211.2 billion to ¥3,147.2 billion, mainly due to an increase in bonds and borrowings. Equity increased by ¥312.9 billion to ¥5,501.8 billion, mainly due to an increase in mark-to-market of investment securities.
