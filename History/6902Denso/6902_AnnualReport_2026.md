1.Summary of Management Results
In light of the external environment, such as the growing severity of social issues, increasing global multi-
polarization and diversification and the change in people’s values, the Group established the 2030 Mid-Term
Management Plan “CORE 2030” as a new guiding compass to continue enhancing customer value. In CORE
2030, the Group defines its vision as "bringing a mobility-driven future to life through human potential." By
positioning its strategic pillars: Strengthening Product Development, Innovating Manufacturing, and Developing
People and Co-Creating with Partners, as the foundation for growth, the Group aims to achieve sustainable growth
that both improves profitability through higher added value and contributes to solving social challenges.
For the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, the global economy maintained a steady underlying performance,
supported by monetary and fiscal policies in major countries and an increase in global AI-related investment,
despite the impact of U.S. tariff policies. On the other hand, uncertainty increased due to geopolitical fragmentation
such as supply chain disruptions and accelerating inflation caused by the situation in the Middle East.
For the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, revenue increased by ¥378.2 billion or 5.3%, year over year, to ¥7,540.0
billion due to an increase in vehicle sales. Operating profit increased by ¥33.6 billion or 6.5%, year over year, to
¥552.5 billion mainly due to efforts of cost reduction and production volume increase, despite the impact of U.S.
tariffs, rising material costs and increased investment in human resources. Profit before income taxes increased by
¥39.3 billion or 6.8%, year over year, to ¥617.3 billion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent company
increased by ¥24.7 billion or 5.9% to ¥443.8 billion.
By geographical segment, revenue in Japan increased by ¥187.7 billion, or 4.5%, year over year, to ¥4,404.1
billion due to an increase in vehicle sales. Operating profit decreased by ¥34.6 billion, or 15.7%, year over year, to
¥185.9 billion mainly due to rising material costs and increased investment in human resources, despite efforts of
cost reduction.
Revenue in North America increased by ¥162.0 billion, or 8.7%, year over year, to ¥2,025.1 billion due to an
increase in vehicle sales. Operating profit increased by ¥34.3 billion, or 34.9%, year over year, to ¥132.3
billion due to efforts of cost reduction and recovery of one-time expenses.
Revenue in Europe increased by ¥49.2 billion, or 6.8%, year over year, to ¥767.9 billion due to a weaker yen and
an increase in vehicle sales. Operating profit increased by ¥19.1 billion, or 221.3%, year over year, to ¥27.8 billion
mainly due to efforts of cost reduction.
Revenue in Asia increased by ¥36.8 billion, or 1.9%, year over year, to ¥1,976.9 billion due to an increase in
vehicle sales. Operating profit increased by ¥9.4 billion, or 5.5%, year over year, to ¥178.8 billion mainly due to
efforts of cost reduction.
Revenue in other regions increased by ¥7.3 billion, or 6.1%, year over year, to ¥126.3 billion. Operating profit
increased by ¥2.6 billion, or 11.7%, year over year, to ¥24.9 billion.

2.Summary of Financial Position
Total assets as of March 31, 2026, increased by ¥605.9 billion, to ¥8,730.9 billion compared with prior year-end
mainly due to an increase in cash and cash equivalents. The total for current and non-current liabilities increased
by ¥78.9 billion, to ¥3,015.0 billion compared with prior year-end mainly due to an increase in bonds and
borrowings. Equity increased by ¥526.9 billion, to ¥5,715.8 billion compared with prior year-end mainly due to an
increase in profit and comprehensive income for the year.
3.Cash flows
In terms of cash flows for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, operating activities provided net cash of
¥511.0 billion, investing activities used net cash of ¥16.9 billion, and financing activities used net cash of
¥355.0 billion. As a result, cash and cash equivalents increased by ¥202.6 billion, to ¥1,189.1 billion.
Net cash provided by operating activities for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026 decreased by ¥247.7
billion from ¥758.7 billion in the previous fiscal year to ¥511.0 billion. This decrease was mainly due to ¥120.7
billion decrease in increase (decrease) in trade payables and ¥92.3 billion increase in income taxes paid, whereas
¥39.3 billion increase in profit before income taxes.
Net cash provided by or used in investing activities decreased by ¥138.8 billion from ¥121.9 billion cash increase
in the previous fiscal year to ¥16.9 billion cash decrease. This decrease was mainly due to ¥55.4 billion paid for
the acquisition of shares in Axia Group B.V., which became the Company’s subsidiary and the ¥37.9 billion
decrease in proceeds from sales of equity instruments.
Net cash used in financing activities decreased by ¥322.4 billion from ¥677.4 billion in the previous fiscal year to
¥355.0 billion. This increase mainly due to the ¥355.4 billion increase in proceeds from borrowings.
Purchases of property, plant and equipment decreased by 2.6% from ¥380.1 billion in the previous fiscal year to
¥370.2 billion. This decrease was due to the promotion of investments while simultaneously strengthening
investments in focused areas and disciplined business operations.
4.Summary of Financial Forecast
In the full-year forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, reflecting the impact of exchange rate
differences due to the assumption of a weaker yen and an increase in vehicle sales, the Group expects revenue to
be increased by ¥130.0 billion, or increased by 1.7%, year over year, to ¥7,670.0 billion.
Operating profit is expected to be decreased by ¥52.5 billion, or decreased by 9.5%, year over year, to ¥500.0
billion reflecting increasing uncertainty, such as the impact of the current situation in the Middle East, in addition to
strengthening investments for future growth. Profit before income taxesis expected to be decreased by ¥64.3
billion, or decreased by 10.4%, year over year, to ¥553.0 billion. Profit for the year decreased by ¥65.5 billion, or
decreased by 13.4%, year over year, to ¥422.0 billion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent company is
expected to be decreased by ¥61.8 billion, or decreased by 13.9% year over year, to ¥382.0 billion.
The exchange rate assumptions are 1 USD = 153 yen and 1 EUR = 180 yen.
The above is included full-year forecast based on information currently available. Actual results may differ
materially from these forecasts due to changes in business operations, exchange rate fluctuations, and other
internal and external factors.

Segment information
(1)Outline of reportable segments
The Group's reportable segments are operating segments, or aggregations of operating segments, which are
components of an entity for which separate financial information is available. Such information is evaluated regularly
by the president of the Company for the purposes of making decisions on how to allocate resources and assessing
performance.
The Group mainly manufactures and sells automotive parts and has directors in charge in Japan, North America,
Europe, and Asia. As independent management units, subsidiaries in each region have developed business activities,
as exemplified by establishment or expansion of manufacturing companies, aiming for optimum production and supply
for orders received through operating activities to regional customers.
The Company is in charge of business activities in Japan. Meanwhile, DENSO INTERNATIONAL AMERICA, INC. is
in charge in the United States of America, Canada, and Mexico as the North America region, DENSO
INTERNATIONAL EUROPE B.V. is in charge in Europe regions (mainly Netherlands, United Kingdom, Italy, Spain,
Hungary, and Czech, etc.), respectively. In Asia (mainly Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, India, Taiwan, China, and
Korea, etc.), DENSO INTERNATIONAL ASIA CO., LTD. (Thailand), DENSO INTERNATIONAL ASIA PTE. LTD.
(Singapore), and DENSO (CHINA) INVESTMENT CO., LTD. have been cooperating together as a management unit
to strengthen the system that is closely linked to the region for both sides of optimal production and supply system.
Since the Group is composed of regional segments based on manufacturing and selling systems, the Group
determined that "Japan," "North America," "Europe," and "Asia" are its reportable segments. The Group has been
manufacturing and selling mainly automotive parts in each reportable segment.
Intersegment transactions are priced with reference to those applicable to transactions with external parties.
Reportable segment profit is measured on the basis of operating profit in the consolidated statement of income.
Finance income, finance costs, foreign exchange gains/losses, share of profit/loss of associates and joint ventures
accounted for using the equity method, and income tax expenses are excluded from the reportable segment profit,
since they are not included in the financial information evaluated by the president of the Company.
