Earnings Call Insights: Ingredion (INGR) Q2 2026
Management View

    "Ingredion delivered a second quarter performance, which was in line with expectations, led by continued momentum in Texture & Healthful Solutions, with net sales increasing 1% to $1.85 billion." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "Quarter 2 marked the ninth consecutive quarter of net sales volume growth in the segment, up 7% with broad-based growth from our solutions offerings and clean label ingredients." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "We did see additional increases in tapioca costs in the quarter with root prices now up more than 40% since the start of the year due to weather-related impacts limiting supply." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "We launched Ask Ingredion, our AI-powered formulation platform designed to help customers identify ingredients and solve formulation challenges that help them bring new products to market faster." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "We also strengthened our healthful solutions portfolio through the acquisition of Benicaros, a clinically supported immune health prebiotic." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "Last week, Tate & Lyle shareholders approved the terms of a recommended all-cash offer by Ingredion for the entire issued and to be issued share capital of Tate & Lyle." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "The financial profile of the transaction remains compelling with the addition of $2.7 billion of highly complementary revenue, the opportunity to deliver $130 million of expected run rate synergies by 2030 and the expectations to deliver greater than 15% adjusted EPS accretion in the first full calendar year post acquisition, all with a clear path to achieving less than 2.5x net leverage within 18 months of closing." (Chairman, President & CEO James Zallie)

    "Net sales for the second quarter were $1.85 billion, up 1% versus prior year." (Interim CFO Jason Payant)

Outlook

    "We expect full year adjusted earnings per share to be in the range of $10.30 to $10.90, in line with previous guidance after reflecting the sale of our majority stake in the Pakistan business." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "For the full year 2026, we still anticipate net sales to be flat to up low single digits, but are now expecting adjusted operating income to be down mid-single digits, reflecting the impact of the sale of the majority stake in the Pakistan business on the second half of the year." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "We anticipate that our 2026 cash from operations will now be in the range of $700 million to $800 million" and "Capital expenditures for the full year are now anticipated to be between $450 million to $490 million with additional spend allocated to our Argo facility." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "For the third quarter of 2026, we expect net sales to be up low single digits and adjusted operating income to be down mid-single digits, which reflects the impact of the sale of the majority stake in our Pakistan business." (Interim CFO Payant)

Financial Results

    "Reported and adjusted operating income were $188 million and $258 million, respectively." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "Adjusted diluted EPS declined $0.05 compared to the prior year, primarily driven by $0.34 of margin impacts." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "Year-to-date cash from operations was $123 million, reflecting a planned investment of approximately $231 million in working capital" and "We invested $210 million of capital expenditures, net of disposals." (Interim CFO Payant)

    "During the first half, we continued to return cash to shareholders through $105 million in dividends and the repurchase of $14 million of shares" and management reaffirmed "our commitment to $100 million of share repurchases this year." (Interim CFO Payant; Chairman, President & CEO Zallie)

Q&A

    Kristen Owen, Oppenheimer: Asked if second-half U.S./Canada guidance implies additional volume/mix headwinds as Argo normalizes; Interim CFO Payant said, "it's really only amending the range to reflect the sale of the majority stake in the Pakistan business," while Chairman, President & CEO Zallie detailed fixes and said refinery downtime and rework are "now been completely addressed" and oil processing is "now operating at historical run rates."

    Kristen Owen, Oppenheimer: Asked about inflation pass-through; Interim CFO Payant said tapioca pass-through "does take about 1 quarter to 1.5 quarters" and estimated Middle East conflict inflation impacts outside tapioca as "in the range of a few million dollars."

    Benjamin Klieve, Benchmark: Pressed on Argo margin structure and reliability investments; Interim CFO Payant said, "we should get back to normal historical margins towards the end of the year," and Chairman, President & CEO Zallie said improvements included "extensive root cause analysis across maintenance, training, leadership and operating procedures" plus targeted redundancy capex.

    Benjamin Theurer, Barclays: Probed U.S./Canada demand vs Argo disruption; Interim CFO Payant said, "the larger share of the impact is from the Argo challenges, not necessarily the weaker demand environment," while Chairman, President & CEO Zallie added, "we did go through -- kind of jump through some hoops to make sure that we service customers."

    Joshua Spector, UBS: Questioned Argo impact math and 2027 baseline; Interim CFO Payant replied, "your math is directionally correct" and said F&II U.S./Canada should return to "normal profit margins and levels next year," while noting temporary intersegment/network effects.

    Andrew Strelzik, BMO: Asked about Tate & Lyle integration planning; Chairman, President & CEO Zallie said, "No real surprises" and emphasized the deal is subject to "regulatory approval in 11 jurisdictions." On potential Canada tariffs, Interim CFO Payant said "the impact on us will be fairly limited" and the company expects to pass through costs.

Sentiment Analysis

    Analyst sentiment was slightly negative to pressing, with repeated challenges on Argo durability and quantification (e.g., Joshua Spector, UBS: "My math looks like...").

    Management sentiment was slightly positive but measured, repeatedly emphasizing operational normalization and process discipline (e.g., Chairman, President & CEO Zallie: "we expect to be operating at normalized run rates for the balance of the year" and "we do feel we've turned the corner").

    Versus Q1, management used more definitive closure language on Argo in Q2 (Q1: "we expect to return to normal operations in this unit within the second quarter"; Q2: "the plant was operating at normal production rates across all major operating units").

Quarter-over-Quarter Comparison

    Guidance language shifted after the Pakistan stake sale: Q1 guided full-year adjusted operating income "flat to down low single digits" and adjusted EPS $10.45 to $11.15; Q2 guided adjusted operating income "down mid-single digits" and adjusted EPS $10.30 to $10.90. (Interim CFO Payant)

    Capex expectations increased in Q2 from $400 million to $440 million (Q1) to $450 million to $490 million (Q2), tied to "additional spend allocated to our Argo facility." (Interim CFO Payant)

    Texture & Healthful Solutions volume momentum accelerated (Q1: "volume growth, up 2%"; Q2: "up 7%"), while Argo commentary progressed from recovery targets in Q1 to normalized rates by end of June in Q2. (Chairman, President & CEO Zallie)

    Strategic narrative broadened in Q2 with announced Benicaros acquisition and the Ask Ingredion launch, alongside Tate & Lyle shareholder approval; Q1 emphasized AI-enabled solution briefs and operational containment at Argo. (Chairman, President & CEO Zallie)

Risks and Concerns

    "Continued macroeconomic pressures in Mexico" and peso-driven transactional impacts remained a key earnings headwind, with management reiterating Mexico’s cost base mismatch. (Chairman, President & CEO Zallie; Interim CFO Payant)

    Tapioca inflation risk increased, with root prices "up more than 40%" year-to-date and a pass-through lag of "1 quarter to 1.5 quarters." (Chairman, President & CEO Zallie; Interim CFO Payant)

    Tariff and geopolitical-related cost volatility remained in guidance assumptions, including "current tariff levels in effect at the end of July 2026" and a "Middle East response team" to manage inflation and logistics impacts. (Interim CFO Payant; Interim CFO Payant)

Final Takeaway

Ingredion management characterized Q2 performance as "in line with expectations," anchored by sustained growth in Texture & Healthful Solutions and improving Argo operations, while reaffirming the Tate & Lyle strategic rationale and financial targets. The company reaffirmed full-year adjusted EPS of $10.30 to $10.90 while highlighting key near-term swing factors investors focused on in Q&A: Argo’s pace of margin normalization, Mexico transactional FX pressure, and the timing lag in passing through elevated tapioca and conflict-related costs.
