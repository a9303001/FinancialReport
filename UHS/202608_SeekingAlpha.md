- **發布時間**: 2026-07-29
- **核心觀點與論述**:
  > "I am initiating coverage on Universal Health Services with a Buy rating and a 24-month investment horizon, citing attractive valuations."
  >
  > "UHS's behavioral health segment offers robust EBITDA margins and geographic diversification, with growth potential from the Talkspace acquisition and UK expansion."
  >
  > "Despite concerns from the OBBBA and recent earnings misses, UHS's strong credit profile, healthy cash flows, and low leverage support continued capital allocation flexibility."
  >
  > "UHS trades at a forward EV/EBITDA of 5.72 versus a sector median of 12.78, indicating a meaningful valuation gap and capped downside."
- **關鍵要點與分析**:
  - **利多（Buy 論述核心）**：① 行為健康（BHS）部門 EBITDA 利潤率佳、地理分散，Talkspace 收購與英國擴張帶來成長；② 信用體質強、現金流健康、槓桿低（淨槓桿 1.8x），支撐持續回購與資本配置彈性；③ 遠期 EV/EBITDA 僅 5.72 倍 vs. 產業中位數 12.78 倍，估值折價巨大、下檔有限。
  - **估值脈絡**：文中指出 UHS 於 2025 年 11 月曾觸及 52 週高點 $246.3，之後大幅修正（8/7 股價約 $173.54），作者視此為「錯殺後的買點」。
  - **主要風險（作者亦點出）**：OBBBA（One Big Beautiful Bill Act）政策風險與近期 earnings miss。
