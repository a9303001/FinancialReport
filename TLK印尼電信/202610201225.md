Summary

    PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk remains a buy, following my evaluation of its recent corporate transaction and near-term prospects.

I've kept PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk (TLK) (TLKM.JK) as a 'Buy-rated' name.

I see TLK's 3Q2026 financials outperforming consensus, after assessing product data and corporate disclosures. The stock could potentially command a richer EV/EBITDA, following a likely monetization of its infrastructure operations.

My prior July 28, 2026, article highlighted the constructive takeaways provided by TLK's latest investor events, including sell-side conferences and the annual shareholder meeting.
Separation Of Digital Infrastructure Assets Is Value-Enhancing

At the end of last month, TLK filed a Form 6-K revealing that it completed the "spin-off of the wholesale fiber connectivity business" into its "controlled company, PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia (TIF)."

According to the local newspaper "Indonesia Business Post," this move is part of a "planned [partial] divestment" that will be explored "through partnerships, capital injections, or other corporate actions."

I've identified several benefits of the carve-out as outlined below.

TIF's asset load factor currently stands at a modest high-20s percentage. As per its Sep 2026 investor deck, TLK intends to position the spun-off entity as a "carrier neutral wholesale fiber provider." My take is that this independence should allow the infrastructure unit to attract more third-party mobile network operators.

The 1H26 EBITDA-to-sales metric for TIF was 54.2%, or 480bps above the group-level figure. Expanding the external customer base should drive up utilization, translating into even higher margins for the spin-off and the parent through fixed-cost leverage.

This recent restructuring unlocks fresh inorganic upside as well.

Earlier in March, Indonesian media outlet Kompas cited a TLK executive's comments suggesting that TIF "has the opportunity to consolidate the fiber optic assets owned by all other state-owned enterprises," which aligns with "[sovereign wealth fund] Danantara's directives."

In my opinion, it makes sense to have a single fiber platform to accelerate "fixed broadband (FBB)" penetration in Indonesia. The FBB take-up rate at the end of last year was just a mid-20s percent.

Also, the spin-off helps preserve parent-level cash flows for stockholder distributions.

As indicated in its 1H2026 results slides, TLK's goal is to maintain its future "capex-to-revenue ratio at 17%-19%." That's comparable with the actual FY22-25 mean of 19.6%.

"The firm projected that its proportion of earnings paid out could surpass 100% in the future," as per my Jul 28 write-up. Telekomunikasi's consensus forward dividend yield is 9.6%, backed by this investor-friendly capital allocation framework. The TIF separation ensures that it's self-funding and won't affect the group's free cash flow generation and shareholder returns.

Looking ahead, a likely sale of TIF's minority interest could serve as a critical pricing benchmark.

TLK is now trading at a depressed forward EV/EBITDA of 3.5 times. In comparison, fiber assets similar to TIF have been sold at EBITDA multiples ranging from a high-single-digit to the mid-teens.

Anticipating A Positive Q3 Surprise

The firm will be disclosing its Jul-Sep '26 results on Wednesday, October 28.

Analysts are forecasting that TLK's EBITDA will rise by 3.4% from IDR18,287B in 3Q2025 to IDR18,916B for 3Q2026. I view the consensus numbers as overly conservative.

My checks on the company's product website suggest that it lifted the selling prices of its daily 1GB/3GB and fortnightly 4GB/8GB wireless plans last month. During the same period, the firm also removed the monthly 13GB/26GB subscriptions.

I believe these actions are consistent with its strategy to sustain healthy growth, implying upside to its third-quarter top-line performance.

TLK's H1 earnings presentation stressed that it will adopt "disciplined pricing, product simplification," with a targeted "migration towards higher-value offerings and cross-selling of digital content services."

Also, the sell-side's 3Q26 estimates point to an assumed EBITDA-to-revenue ratio of 49.6%. This contrasts with management's objective of registering a 50%+ EBITDA margin for the current fiscal year.

TLK indicated in its Sep 2026 investor slides that it "divested 2 and closed 8 subsidiaries" as part of "streamlining initiatives" during 1H26. I think the firm shouldn't have many issues meeting its operating profitability goal. The proactive portfolio restructuring would have eliminated low-return business units.

The enterprise is shrinking its workforce to save on labor expenses too. The same investment deck published last month mentioned "lower employee benefit expenses following the Early Retirement Program."

I'm predicting a better-than-expected Q3 EBITDA of IDR19.2T for TLK, implying a 5.0% growth rate.
Spotlight On Potential Index Removal

Telekomunikasi's primary place of operations is its home market, Indonesia.

Reuters reported yesterday that MSCI is "laying out a plan to remove it [Indonesia] from a suite of indexes tracked by funds."

It's likely that TLK could experience substantial fund outflows, if this above-mentioned bear-case scenario materializes.

But I see this as a short-term, non-fundamental-related risk.

Certain index-hugging institutional investors could have to sell the stock and other Indonesian names. I believe this doesn't change the underlying business and financial prospects of the company. Also, the firm's telecom-related operations are expected to be more defensive than other economically-sensitive or cyclical enterprises in the country.
You Need To Contemplate These Variant Views

TIF may fail to attract external customers or ramp up inorganic growth post-spin. A dimmer outlook could translate into a lower monetization value.

TLK might execute poorly on price optimization and cost control measures. This increases the risk of earnings misses in subsequent quarters.
My Closing Remarks

I'm still optimistic about TLK's prospects as a potential investment candidate.

I continue to use Indonesian telecom player XLSMART (PTXKY) for my peer comparison exercise. PTXKY's next twelve months' EV/EBITDA is 4.6x, or 31% (4.6/3.5) above the same for TLK.

The stock remains undervalued, with re-rating triggers such as a 3Q2026 beat and the sale of a partial stake in TIF at favorable multiples.
