Telkom Indonesia

Yohanes Hasiholan/iStock Editorial via Getty Images
Yohanes Hasiholan/iStock 社論來自 Getty Images

I'm revising my rating for PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk (TLK) (TLKM.JK) from 'Hold' to 'Buy.' There are encouraging read-throughs from the recently-concluded spectrum auction, regulatory updates, and TLK's own stockholder meeting.
我已將印尼電信公司 (PT Telekomunikasi Indonesia Tbk，股票代碼： TLK ) (TLKM.JK) 的評級從「持有」調整為「買入」。近期結束的頻譜拍賣、監管更新以及 TLK 自身的股東大會都釋放出令人鼓舞的訊號。

Its below-expectations FY25 EPS and value-enhancing asset monetization were detailed in the previous May 14, 2026, update.
其低於預期的 2025 財年每股收益和增值資產貨幣化已在 2026 年 5 月 14 日的更新中詳細說明 。
Favorable Airwaves Bidding Outcome
有利的頻譜競標結果

My mid-May article indicated that "concerns regarding a costly 4G-to-5G transition have been allayed with a lowered first-year upfront payment for spectrum awardees." This optimistic stance has been validated by the actual allocation exercise.
我在五月中旬的文章中指出，「由於頻譜分配方首年預付款降低，人們對 4G 向 5G 過渡成本高昂的擔憂已經得到緩解。」實際的頻譜分配工作也證實了這一樂觀觀點。

The Jakarta Globe reported last week that "Indonesia has awarded radio spectrum in the 700 MHz and 2.6 GHz bands to the country's three largest mobile operators." These are TLK, Indosat (PTITF), and XLSMART (PTXKY).
《雅加達環球報》 上週報道稱，「印尼已將 700 MHz 和 2.6 GHz 頻段的無線電頻譜授予該國三大行動營運商。」這三大營運商分別是 TLK、Indosat（ PTITF ）和 XLSMART（ PTXKY ）。

I'm reassured by the absence of hyper-competitive over-bidding. The cost/MHz for the latest frequency assignment was between 42% and 67% cheaper than that of prior rounds, based on my calculations.
令我感到欣慰的是，沒有出現過度競爭的競標現象。根據我的計算，最新一輪頻率分配的每兆赫成本比前幾輪降低了 42% 到 67%。

Also, I estimate TLK's annualized bandwidth fee payments to be around IDR 1.2T. That's equivalent to a modest 2% of the firm's trailing twelve months' EBITDA. It shouldn't have any problems funding this, given its healthy net gearing of 0.19x.
此外，我估計 TLK 的年度頻寬費用約為 1.2 兆印尼盾。這僅相當於該公司過去 12 個月 EBITDA 的 2%。鑑於其 0.19 倍的健康淨負債率，該公司應該能夠輕鬆支付這筆費用。

In my opinion, the company retains an edge over rivals as well. Post-auction, the aggregate spectrum for TLK amounts to roughly 265MHz. This exceeds PTITF's and PTXKY's respective holdings by 23% and 22%.
我認為，該公司在競爭中仍保持優勢。拍賣後，TLK 的總頻譜約為 265MHz，比 PTITF 和 PTXKY 各自的頻譜持有量分別高出 23% 和 22%。

The group secured the biggest share (80MHz) among the bidders for the 2.6 GHz frequencies too. That plays a critical role in boosting its mid-band 5G capacity.
該集團在 2.6GHz 頻段的競標中也獲得了最大份額（80MHz）。這對於提升其 5G 中頻段容量至關重要。
Promising Capital Return Outlook

At its Annual General Meeting last month, TLK secured stockholder authorization for its dividend and buyback proposals.

Its FY25 cash distribution of IDR 222/share represented a 4.5% hike. The 97% implied payout ratio on a normalized earnings basis was a substantial improvement from FY24's 89%.

This also translated into an appealing 8.6% trailing yield for the stock. That compares favorably with the three-year historical mean of 6.2%.

Separately, stockholders also approved a fresh IDR 4T one-year repurchase program that started on June 9. It means that TLK could possibly acquire up to 1.6% of its outstanding share base over the next 12 months.

Looking ahead, I think it's likely that the company will continue to reward investors in a generous manner.

Earlier, TLK took part in UBS's Asian Investment Conference in late-May. According to the bank's proprietary June 1 report titled 2026 AIC, management has the intent to "allocate asset sale proceeds to a dividend pool for special distributions over the coming three years." The firm projected that its proportion of earnings paid out could surpass 100% in the future.

TLK's AIC event remarks align with my May 14 piece highlighting that TLK is "running a divestment process for eight other non-core units."

As per S&P Capital IQ, the stock boasts a consensus FY2027 dividend yield of 8.2%, assuming a 95% payout. This metric improves to an even more enticing 8.7% if the above-mentioned full earnings distribution target is met.
Regulatory Changes Should Bolster TLK's Financial Prospects

A recent commentary published in Indonesian media Kompas highlighted the nation's "biometric-based SIM card registration policy that has been implemented since July 1, 2026." A major distinction is that the new data verification fee of IDR 3,000 represents a threefold increase compared to the legacy identity-linked framework.

In the past, Indonesian consumers were heavily reliant on multi-SIM acquisitions to exploit cheaper short-term promotional rates. From my perspective, that naturally led to elevated user churn and intense price rivalry among operators.

I anticipate a growing number of domestic subscribers to maintain their existing cards, rather than deal with the administrative hassle and cost of biometric re-registration. My take is that TLK should possess greater leverage to charge more for its wireless bundles with limited volume trade-offs.

According to S&P Capital IQ, analysts are forecasting that TLK's EBITDA-to-sales metric will widen from 49.2% in FY2025 to 49.7% and 49.8% for FY26 and FY27, respectively. This is consistent with my view that the company's operating profitability will most probably be enhanced by the updated legislative directives.
You Shouldn't Ignore These Downside Factors

A slower-than-expected pace of divestitures could imply lower one-off distributions. That will disappoint yield-hunting investors and compel them to seek other higher-return alternatives.

TLK is required to implement 5G coverage for over half of Indonesia's population by 2031 as part of the spectrum award conditions. Its mid-to-long term cash flows might be under pressure. This assumes that 5G-related commercial adoption is underwhelming, which leads to poor economies of scale.
My Conclusion

I've become a bull on the stock.

TLK's, PTITF's, and PTXKY's consensus next twelve months' EV/EBITDAs are 4.1x, 3.9x, and 5.0x, respectively (source: S&P Capital IQ).

As I indicated above, Telekomunikasi is the leading player with regards to spectrum holdings. It should trade at parity with, if not at a premium to, its listed domestic competitors.

Benchmarked against PTXKY, TLK's multiple expansion implies a re-rating potential of at least +22% (5.0x/4.1x). This justifies a 'Buy' rating for this name. I have already identified catalysts such as improved margins and more generous distributions.
