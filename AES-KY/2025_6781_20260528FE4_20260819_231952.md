### **Chapter I.Letter to Shareholders Business Report** 

#### **I. 2025 Business Report** 

(I) Results of Business Plan Implementation 

#### 1. Business Results: 

The Company has long been dedicated to the research and development, manufacturing, and sales of professional lithium battery module technologies. In 2025, the Company recorded revenue of NT$16,000,812 thousand, representing an increase of 59.4% compared with the previous year. The growth was mainly attributable to the strong demand for industrial lithium battery modules, particularly those used in medium-to-large industrial backup power systems. 

Against the backdrop of carbon neutrality, the global electrification of light mobility vehicles continues to accelerate. Electric bicycles, electric scooters, and electric motorcycles remain popular and have become representative modes of transportation for short-distance travel. As a result, the electric two-wheeler market continues to demonstrate a stable growth trend. The sustained increase in demand has attracted a large number of manufacturers to enter the market, driving technological advancements and product upgrades, while also intensifying market competition. At the same time, ongoing global political and economic uncertainties, rising geopolitical risks, declining purchasing power due to high inflation, and continued inventory adjustments by brand suppliers have collectively impacted the Company’s sales performance in 2025. 

In the market for battery modules used in medium-to-large industrial backup power systems, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and big data have become the fundamental pillars of the intelligent world. These developments are driving demand for information exchange through data centers, edge computing, and storage systems, thereby accelerating the deployment of data centers worldwide. Brand server manufacturers, networking equipment providers, and power service companies are actively investing in the development of data center-related architectures. Lithium battery backup power modules have become an industry trend, with increasing requirements for both power capacity and energy capacity. The Company has cultivated this market segment for many years and has achieved meaningful progress in both research and development and market expansion, continuing to contribute positively to the Company’s operational performance in 2025. 

In 2026, in addition to increasing shipment volumes to existing customers, the Company will allocate more resources toward the development of new customers and new products in the industrial control battery module segment.

- (II) Sales forecast and its basis 

Global sales of light electric mobility vehicles are experiencing rapid growth. In 2025, the global market size for light electric vehicles is estimated to range between approximately US$91.2 billion and US$98.2 billion. By 2030, the global light electric vehicle market is projected to reach approximately US$140 billion, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of around 9.7%. According to market estimates, the global electric bicycle (e-bike) market reached approximately US$35 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow to approximately US$62.25 billion by 2030, representing a compound annual growth rate of nearly 10% during the period. Nevertheless, the current market environment still faces several structural challenges, including excess inventory and other market adjustments. These factors have resulted in a slower-than-expected pace of market recovery and have introduced additional uncertainties to the industry . 

Behind the continued development of the market, the industry is also facing increasing challenges. Manufacturers are encountering multiple pressures, including overcapacity, rising costs, inventory accumulation, and liquidity constraints. Although overall market demand remains present, the entry of numerous manufacturers has resulted in oversupply, leading the electric two- 

3 

wheeler industry into a period characterized by elevated inventory levels. After a prolonged period of promotional activities and inventory adjustments, the industry has gradually begun to recover from the high inventory situation. However, economic pressures, changes in consumer behavior, and evolving market dynamics continue to pose challenges for many well-known brands. Under such circumstances, the Company will continue to strengthen the development of new battery products and related peripheral products. At the same time, the Company will deepen cooperation with existing customers and collaborate with leading system integrators in the industry to create new growth momentum for future operations. 

With the ongoing deployment of 5G base stations and the rapid development of artificial intelligence technologies, infrastructure construction for base stations and data centers continues to expand, thereby driving demand for battery modules used in medium-to-large industrial backup power systems. In particular, there is an increasing trend of replacing traditional lead-acid batteries with lithium batteries in base station backup applications. Furthermore, the rapid development of artificial intelligence has significantly increased the demand for electricity in data centers, making backup power systems an important driver for the next stage of industry growth. The Company has also made concrete progress in acquiring new customers in this market segment. It is expected that the sales volume and proportion of lithium batteries used in data center and 5G base station backup power applications will continue to increase in 2026. 

Over the years, the Company has actively cultivated the lithium battery module market and expanded applications across various new energy sectors. Currently, the Company’s primary revenue source is battery modules for light electric mobility vehicles. Looking ahead, as environmental awareness continues to grow, the light electric mobility vehicle market is expected to maintain its growth momentum. In addition, driven by increasing demand for cloud computing, 5G networks, and edge computing, revenue from battery modules used in medium-to-large industrial backup power systems has also shown growth. 

The Company will continue to devote more resources to develop new customers and new product lines, to scale up production automation to enhance operational efficiency. The Company aspires to become a key member in the green energy supply chain, and to make our share of contributions to a better future on our shared planet.

#### 1-1 Greenhouse Gas Inventory and Assurance Status for the Most Recent Two Years 

#### 1-1-1 Greenhouse Gas Inventory Information 

The Company discloses its greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions (metric tons of CO₂e), emission intensity (metric tons of CO₂e per NT$ million), and data coverage scope for the most recent two years. 

On May 8, 2024, the Company obtained an ISO 14064-1:2018 greenhouse gas verification statement for 2023 issued by an independent third-party verification body, SGS. The verified GHG inventory covered the Company’s major sites (Zhaopu, Jiapu, and Trend Power Changshu), including Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions. Total GHG emissions amounted to 2,099.61 metric tons of CO₂e, with an emission intensity of 0.21 (metric tons of CO₂e per NT$ million). 

On May 23, 2025, the Company obtained an ISO 14064-1:2018 greenhouse gas verification statement for 2024 issued by SGS. The verified GHG inventory covered the Company’s major sites (Zhaopu, Jiapu, and Trend Power Changshu), including Scope 1 and Scope 2 (market-based) emissions. Total GHG emissions amounted to 1,802.60 metric tons of CO₂e, with an emission intensity of 0.18 (metric tons of CO₂e per NT$ million).

#### (II) Industrial Overview: 

1. Current situation and development of the industry 

The industrial scale of batteries can be divided into primary batteries and secondary batteries. Secondary batteries refer to batteries that can be recharged after the power consumption is finished. If the service life of the battery ends after the power consumption, it is called a primary battery. According to the original distinction, secondary batteries can be divided into lead-acid batteries (PB), nickel-hydrogen batteries (NiMh), nickel-cadmium batteries (NiCd) and lithium secondary batteries (LIB). 

The lead-acid battery (PB) occupies the largest market share because of its mature technology and low price. With the evolution and development of portable electronic products, lithium battery (LIB) has become the mainstream of power secondary batteries in recent years with its advantages of high capacity, small size and light weight, and its market size is second only to lead-acid battery (PB). Under the influence of environmental protection and energy sustainability issues, the demand of electric vehicles and energy storage industry will gradually increase.
