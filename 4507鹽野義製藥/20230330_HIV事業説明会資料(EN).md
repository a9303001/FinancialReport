### **Integrase Inhibitor “Backbone” Leads HIV Market** 

###### **Basic design of highly active anti-HIV therapy***<sup>**1**</sup> 

- Efficiently suppress HIV replication by combining multiple anti-HIV drugs with different mechanism of action 

- Anti-HIV drugs require long-term clinical evidence due to lifelong administration 

**Integrase inhibitors account for 70% of HIV market share in 9 major regions***<sup>**5**</sup> 

###### **Characteristics of integrase inhibitor regimens** 

- Highly ranked global guidelines*<sup>2-4</sup> 

- Extensive clinical and real-world evidence supporting longterm efficacy, safety and high genetic barrier
